# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People who need an Arabic–German language service for official, medical, legal, or personal matters. They often arrive under time pressure and need a clear path to request a certified translation or arrange interpreting.

## Product Purpose

Shaker Consulting makes certified Arabic–German translations and interpreting easy to request, understand, and trust. The primary conversion is a translation inquiry; interpreting is the second service pillar and MPU preparation is a focused supporting offer.

## Brand Personality

Official, personal, dignified. The experience should feel calm and clear rather than bureaucratic or intimidating.

## Anti-references

Do not look like a generic translation marketplace, an immigration-law firm, or a dark hospitality template. Avoid noisy flags, clichéd global imagery, and unsubstantiated legal or medical claims.

## Design Principles

1. Make the official task feel manageable.
2. Lead with Arabic–German certified translations, not a list of every possible service.
3. Pair institutional credibility with personal accessibility.
4. Treat Arabic as an equal, complete language experience.
5. Explain the next step before asking for an inquiry.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast. Support keyboard navigation and reduced motion. Arabic content must use native right-to-left layout and clear, readable typography.

## Open legal work

- Impressum and privacy policy content must still be supplied by the website operator and published before a compliant go-live. Tracked in `LEGAL.md`.
- All site imagery is AI-generated and must remain clearly labelled for users.
